Rendered from the device's own data: /api/ui/profile/radio geometry, real LVGL font metrics, the active theme palette and the WebSocket state feed. Read-only — nothing here writes to the device.

Dashed boxes are placeholders, not drawn artwork: the bar VU, stereo bars, cassette wheels and the mode/event indicators. Everything else — text, background plates, wallpaper, volume slider, station icon — is drawn the way the firmware draws it. The clock reads this browser's time, the weather row shows sample text, and long station/title text is clipped here instead of scrolling.

Touch hotspots draw nothing at rest, because that is what the panel does — the wallpaper is the button, the hotspot is only the touch area over it. Hover one to see its real pressed highlight, or tick Show touch areas to outline them all. Hovering does not send anything to the device.

The needle meters are real geometry — pivot, needle length and plate come straight out of vu_needle_widget.c — but their deflection is staged. The RMS levels driving them are tapped inside the DSP element on the device and are not published to any client, so the needles are parked at a representative reading instead of following the music.
