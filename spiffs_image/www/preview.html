About this preview

Screen preview

Rendered from the device's own data: /api/ui/profile/<section> geometry, real LVGL font metrics, the active theme palette and the WebSocket state feed. Source switches between the radio pair (radio + playlist) and the SD pair (player + browser), the same pairing the device makes between a source and its browser.

The page writes to the device. Touch hotspots drive the radio or the SD player exactly as pressing the panel would; list rows play their station or file; the volume bar under the panel sets the plain 0–100 master volume, so <screen>_volslider_vol_max (which only remaps the on-screen slider's travel) does not apply to it. Nothing else here writes.

Touch hotspots draw nothing at rest, because that is what the panel does — the wallpaper is the button, the hotspot is only the touch area over it. Holding one down shows its real pressed highlight, and only then. To check a hotspot against artwork painted into the wallpaper, tick Show touch areas. Radio hotspots use explicit radio commands so they cannot act on the wrong source, and volume steps by 2 like the panel's own path, not by the 5 of the semantic volp frame. On the SD screen play/stop routes through the source-aware toggle/stop frames, because sd_player_stop_keep() has no JSON command of its own — exact while SD is the active source. The three navigation actions (playlist, SD browser, equalizer) move the panel's own UI and have no equivalent on the wire, so those hotspots are shown but inert.

Lists scroll — wheel, or drag them like a finger; a drag that turns into a scroll cancels the pick, exactly as a swipe off a row does on the panel. Rows are ordered as the device orders them: favourites first in the playlist, and up-entry then folders then tracks in the browser. The browser's row icons are stand-ins — LV_SYMBOL_* are FontAwesome glyphs that are not in the Montserrat subset this page ships.

Dashed boxes are placeholders, not drawn artwork: the bar VU, stereo bars and the mode/event indicators. The cassette reels are a faint stand-in rather than a mirror — animated_wheels_widget.c is a thin adapter over a prebuilt static library, so the drawing code cannot be read; only the square geometry and the four palette entries it is handed are known, and the mark is built from those. Everything else — text, background plates, wallpaper, volume slider, station icon, album cover — is drawn the way the firmware draws it. Each screen uses its own wallpaper field, not the radio's.

One thing cannot be truthful and is staged rather than omitted so the layout still reads: the needle meters' deflection. The RMS levels driving them are tapped inside the DSP element and are not published to any client. Their geometry, colours and visibility rules are real. The SD player's counter and progress bar are live — the device publishes sd_position_ms/sd_duration_ms and this page extrapolates between broadcasts, as the protocol doc prescribes; against firmware that predates those fields they fall back to a staged position. The clock reads this browser's time, the weather row shows sample text, and long text is clipped here instead of scrolling.
