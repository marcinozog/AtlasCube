⚠ FORMAT SD CARD
This erases the entire card and writes an empty FAT filesystem. There is no undo and nothing is backed up first.
  • All music, playlists and station icons — gone
  • All wallpapers, layout presets and splash logos — gone
  • Voice notifications and slideshow photos — gone
  • Playback from the card stops immediately
The radio stays blocked while it works — about two minutes for a 32 GB card, longer for a bigger one. Do not power the device off and do not close this page until it reports back.
