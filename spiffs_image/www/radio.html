About this preview

Radio screen — live preview

Rendered from the device's own data: /api/ui/profile/radio geometry, real LVGL font metrics, the active theme palette and the WebSocket state feed. The touch hotspots are live — clicking one drives the radio for real, exactly as pressing the panel would. The volume bar under the panel writes too; it is the plain 0–100 master volume, so radio_volslider_vol_max (which only remaps the on-screen slider's travel) does not apply to it. Nothing else on this page writes to the device.

Show playlist screen reveals SCREEN_PLAYLIST below, drawn from /api/ui/profile/playlist and the live station list. Rows are ordered favourites-first like the device orders them, the cursor sits on the station that is playing, and that station's row carries the accent colour. Its wallpaper is its own playlist_wallpaper, not the radio's. Rows are clickable and play their station, and like the panel they do nothing when that station is already playing. The list scrolls — wheel, or drag it like a finger; a drag that turns into a scroll cancels the pick, exactly as a swipe off a row does on the panel. Changing station re-centres the cursor, again as the panel does. In fullscreen the playlist comes up with the radio and the volume bar, since the toggle button is outside the fullscreen element.

Dashed boxes are placeholders, not drawn artwork: the bar VU, stereo bars, cassette wheels and the mode/event indicators. Everything else — text, background plates, wallpaper, volume slider, station icon — is drawn the way the firmware draws it. The clock reads this browser's time, the weather row shows sample text, and long station/title text is clipped here instead of scrolling.

Touch hotspots draw nothing at rest, because that is what the panel does — the wallpaper is the button, the hotspot is only the touch area over it. Holding one down shows its real pressed highlight, and only then — the panel lights the button while the finger is on it, not after. To check a hotspot against the artwork painted into the wallpaper, tick Show touch areas: that outlines them all and lights them on hover. Clicking sends the command. Play/stop, next, previous and volume reproduce what the panel's own hotspot does, arithmetic included — volume steps by 2, not by the 5 of the semantic volp frame. The three navigation actions (playlist, SD browser, equalizer) move the panel's own UI and have no equivalent on the wire, so those hotspots are shown but inert.

The needle meters are real geometry — pivot, needle length and plate come straight out of vu_needle_widget.c — but their deflection is staged. The RMS levels driving them are tapped inside the DSP element on the device and are not published to any client, so the needles are parked at a representative reading instead of following the music.
